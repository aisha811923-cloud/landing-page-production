### Headline & Lead

- **Subtitle:** *No infinite retakes. No algorithmic feeds. Just the genuine magic of 35mm analog film, re-engineered for the modern era.*

---

### Feature Modules

#### 1. The Blind Viewfinder Engine
Modern phone cameras force you to look at the screen twice: once to take the shot, and five more times to judge, edit, and retake it. EMULSION locks the exposure the moment you click the shutter. There is no preview screen, no delete button, and no retakes. You take the photo, put your phone away, and stay present in the moment.

#### 2. Flexible Roll Architecture (4 to 24 Exposures)
Not every memory needs 24 exposures. You choose the exact roll size for your occasion:
- **4 EXP — The Moment:** 15-minute quick captures for coffee dates, sunsets, and spontaneous catchups.
- **8 EXP — The Evening:** Intimate dinners, concerts, and night strolls.
- **12 EXP — The Weekend:** Road trips, city getaways, and 2-person Roll Swaps.
- **16 EXP — The Story:** Festivals, multi-day celebrations, and group vacations.
- **24 EXP — The Classic 35mm:** Full archival rolls for deep monthly memories.

#### 3. The Roll Swap (Shared Rolls)
Start a shared roll with a friend. If you choose a 12-shot roll, you get 6 shots and they get 6 shots. Both of you shoot completely blind. Neither of you sees what the other captured until the entire roll is filled and developed together.

#### 4. The 4×6 Contact Sheet (Viral Growth Engine)
When your roll finishes developing, EMULSION automatically composites all your exposures into a stylized, high-resolution darkroom contact sheet. Bordered with authentic film rebate markings (`KODAK PORTRA 400`, frame markers, and timestamp), it is ready for 1-tap export directly to your Instagram Story.

#### 5. Collaborative Scrapbook Boards
A shared visual space where you and your close circle can pin developed prints, polaroids, and full contact strips. Organize memories by season, trip, or friend group without public metrics, likes, or follower counts.

# Page 3: Our Story & Founding Team (`/our-story`)

### Headline & Lead
- **Title:** *Why We Built EMULSION*
- **Subtitle:** *We got tired of social media feeling like a staged performance. Here is how four friends decided to bring the anticipation of real film back.*

---

### The Origin Story
One evening, the four of us were sitting around discussing how exhausting modern social media had become. Feeds felt less like real memories and more like a staged competition—taking 50 shots just to post one, spending hours editing, and stressing over instant likes.

While researching user discussions and complaints across Reddit communities, we realized millions of people felt the exact same fatigue. Everyone missed the effortless magic of disposable cameras: shooting without overthinking, not obsessing over previews, and waiting with excitement to see how the roll turned out.

We decided to bring that feeling back. EMULSION was born to replace screen anxiety with shared anticipation—giving you authentic 35mm film rolls, blind shooting, delayed development, and shared scrapbooks with your closest friends.

---

### Our Vision Manifesto
- **Blind Capture:** Take the photo and put your phone away. No retakes, no tweaking, no screen fixation.
- **Delayed Gratification:** Waiting for your roll to develop makes the reveal ten times more meaningful than an instant upload.
- **Shared Keepsakes:** Memories are meant to be kept with the people who were there, not broadcasted to an algorithm.
